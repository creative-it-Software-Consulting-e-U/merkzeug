# Merkzeug privacy policy — draft for publisher review

Status: not published. Confirm the publisher's legal/contact details, dependency review and final features before using this text as a policy. This draft describes the current iOS app and proposed Mac App Store edition; Git/calendar paragraphs must match the final Mac build.

## Your notes

Merkzeug opens and edits Markdown documents in folders you choose. It does not operate a Merkzeug account service or upload your notebook to a Merkzeug-operated server. Files remain in the selected location. Choosing iCloud Drive or another file provider can cause that provider to synchronize your files under its own settings and privacy policy.

## Connections you choose

External images referenced by a note may be downloaded from their source, which receives the network request. Opening an external link contacts the linked service through your browser or another application. If Git integration is offered in your installed edition, Git operations contact the remote repository you configure. A separate application such as Working Copy handles its own Git connections and credentials.

## Calendar access

If your edition offers calendar-based meeting notes, it requests permission to read calendar information configured on your computer. Details you choose to insert become part of the resulting Markdown note and follow that note's storage and synchronization settings.

## Diagnostics and support

The app currently includes no configured Merkzeug analytics or advertising service. Apple's optional diagnostics and store services are governed by Apple's settings and policies. If you contact support, the information and attachments you send are used to address your request. Send a minimal fictional example rather than confidential notes whenever possible.

## Control and deletion

Revoke folder/calendar access in the relevant system settings or choose another folder in Merkzeug. Removing the app does not delete documents you stored outside its private app container. Delete notes through the relevant storage provider if you want to remove them and any provider-managed copies.

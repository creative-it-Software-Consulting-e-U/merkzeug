# One step at a time

## Prepare

- [x] Choose a sunny spot
- [x] List the tools we already have
- [ ] Pick a date for the first garden afternoon

## Make it welcoming

Bring a notebook, a few seeds and an idea. No gardening experience required.

| Bring | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Seeds | Start something new |
| Labels | Remember what we planted |
| A notebook | Keep what we learn |

## Keep learning

At the end of each visit, add a short note: what worked, what changed, and what to try next.

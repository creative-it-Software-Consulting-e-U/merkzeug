01/06your-files.mdStart where you are

One folder. Your whole collection.

A vault is simply a folder of Markdown files. Your notebook, your cloud folder, or the documentation beside your code.

Local & in the cloud
Open existing files without importing them into a new database. Your chosen cloud service handles synchronization.
Git, your way
Use repository folders on Mac and Linux or directly in IntelliJ. On iPhone and iPad, open your Git app’s repository folder through the Files app.
Open files
Markdown stays Markdown. Work on the same files with other editors, Git tools, and agents.
Git on iPhone and iPad: your choice, with direct integration

Working Copy is a separate Git app for iPhone and iPad. It clones repositories and handles version control and synchronization; Merkzeug reads and edits the Markdown files inside them.

You can also use any other Git app that makes its repository folder available for opening and editing through the Files app. Perform Git operations in that app. Working Copy additionally has a direct integration: once configured, you can start Pull, Commit, and Push from Merkzeug.

03/06your-layout.mdFrom draft to delivery

Your Markdown. Your finished document.

Turn a collection of notes into a document for clients, teams, or your next meeting. With your letterhead, your typography, and your layout.

One layout, many uses
Use HTML/CSS templates while editing and when printing. Mac, Linux, iPhone, iPad, and IntelliJ also export PDF files with those templates.
Several notes, one PDF
On Mac, Linux, iPhone, iPad, and in IntelliJ, an overview document can include directly linked Markdown files from its own folder and subfolders in the export.
Share your templates
Sync templates between Apple devices through iCloud, or keep them inside your vault. Choose the location directly in the PDF controls; the shared assignment travels with your Git repository. Share with your team →

The details make the document.

Fine-tune exports with frontmatter: title, language, table of contents, and excluded notes. Your Markdown remains the source.

---
title: Garden project
lang: en
toc: true
---

04/06assets-and-agents.mdKeep things together

Notes evolve. Their connections stay.

Project.md ↔ Project.assets/
Images beside the note
Inserted images live in a companion .assets folder. Move or rename a note with Merkzeug and its assets follow; references are updated.
One heading, one filename
On Mac and Linux, filenames can follow the first main heading. Meeting notes on Mac, Linux, iPhone, and iPad also follow their heading when it changes. IntelliJ retains control of filenames.
Refactoring included
Merkzeug updates local Markdown links when notes are renamed or moved. The IntelliJ plugin also adds the asset rules to the IDE’s Move/Rename actions. Images & refactoring →

Agents contribute. You keep the overview.

Make a readable collection out of agent-written Markdown files. Merkzeug helps you review, edit, and share the result.

A prompt for your layout
Give your agent a template prompt with the file structure, available fields, and styling rules, so it can adapt a template to your visual identity.
Pass on the rules
Review the asset guidance and existing AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md files. Choose which files receive the instructions. Merkzeug itself does not run agents. Template prompts & agent guidance →

For your everyday work, too

From calendar event to meeting note.

On Mac, iPhone, or iPad, choose an event and start with a title, time, and attendees instead of an empty page. On Linux, use ICS files or HTTPS/Webcal subscriptions. Your calendar stays unchanged. Personal notes and ideas need neither Git nor an agent.

Your notes on Linux.

Choose your processor architecture first, then the package for your distribution. Each download contains one architecture only. Updates are installed manually. If unsure, run uname -m: x86_64 means Intel/AMD, aarch64 means ARM64.

AppImageDEB · Ubuntu / DebianRPM · Fedora

For ARM Linux, including Linux in Parallels on Apple Silicon Macs.

AppImage requires FUSE 2 and permission to use unprivileged user namespaces. On systems that restrict this, use DEB or RPM. DEB and RPM install through your package manager. Use local folders, Git repositories, or folders mounted by your sync provider; automatic iCloud discovery is not available on Linux. SHA-256 · Installation and help
